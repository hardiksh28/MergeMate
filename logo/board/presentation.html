What we heard

The brief

MergeMate finds open-source issues that fit a developer's skills, explains the codebase like a senior dev, preps the fix with them, and turns merged PRs into proof recruiters read. The mark should feel friendly, confident and dev-native, and avoid the stock git-branch icon every dev tool uses.

Success: ownable among dev tools, holds at 16 px, works in one colour, carries the existing lime + ink equity.
